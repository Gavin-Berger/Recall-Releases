Recall · Alpha and beta releases

**Remember what happened in your Ableton session.** Recall records the shape of a session as you work, then gives you a timeline of changes and a place to mark the moments worth keeping. It is a desktop companion for producers using Ableton Live.

This repository is the **download and update channel for Recall's closed alpha**. It contains installers, update files, and release notes for invited testers. Development happens in a separate repository.

> **Invited testing:** Recall is still being tested with a small group of producers. Expect rough edges, and please use it on work you can afford to back up.

## Get Recall

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Gavin-Berger/Recall-Releases/releases/latest).
2. Download the installer for your computer from **Assets**:
   - **Windows:** the `.exe` installer.
   - **Mac:** the `.dmg` matching your processor, if a Mac build is listed. Choose **aarch64** for Apple Silicon or **x64** for Intel.
3. Install and open Recall. The in-app setup will help install the Recall control surface in Ableton Live. Restart Ableton, then select **Recall** in **Preferences → Link, Tempo & MIDI → Control Surface**. Leave its MIDI input and output set to **None**.
4. Open a saved Ableton set. Recall should show **Listening to Ableton** when the connection is ready.

Use only the assets attached to a release here or a build sent to you directly by the Recall team. Early builds are not yet signed with a Windows publisher certificate or notarized by Apple, so your operating system may ask you to confirm the first launch. If you see a warning you do not understand, ask us before overriding it.

## What Recall does with your work

Recall keeps its session history on your computer. It does not upload your projects, audio, notes, or captured events automatically, and it does not write changes back to your Ableton sets. You can inspect a problem report before choosing to send it.

The app checks this repository for updates. Updates are signed and verified by the app before installation. The signature protects the update's integrity; access to the download is controlled separately.

## Help shape the alpha

Use Recall during a normal session, then compare its timeline and report with what you remember doing. We especially want to hear when a change is attributed to the wrong track or a session appears incomplete. In Recall, choose **Report a problem** to review and send a report, or share it with the person who invited you to test.

When reporting a bug, include your operating system, Ableton Live version, Recall version, what you expected, and what happened. Please review the report and any screenshot for private project names before sending.

## A note for testers

Early access is built on trust. Please keep shared builds and testing discussions within the invited group, and ask before redistributing an installer or posting screenshots. Thank you for helping make Recall useful for real music work.
