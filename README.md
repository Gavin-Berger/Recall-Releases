# Recall · Beta releases

**A studio workspace for Ableton Live.** Recall keeps every version of your sets, the time you spend on each song, and the releases you build from them, in one place on your computer.

Recall is in **open beta**. Anyone can download it.

## Get Recall

Download Recall from **[recallstudios.net/download](https://recallstudios.net/download/)**. It picks the right installer for your computer.

Then:

1. Install and open Recall. The in-app setup installs the Recall control surface in Ableton Live.
2. Restart Ableton, then select **Recall** in **Preferences → Link, Tempo & MIDI → Control Surface**. Leave its MIDI input and output set to **None**.
3. Open a saved Ableton set. Recall shows **Listening to Ableton** when the connection is ready.

Installers are signed: Windows shows Recall's publisher and the Mac app is notarized by Apple.

## What Recall does with your work

Recall keeps its history on your computer. It does not upload your projects, audio, notes or captured events, and it never writes changes back to your Ableton sets. You can read a problem report before choosing to send it.

This repository is Recall's update channel: the app checks it for new versions, and verifies every update's signature before installing it.

## Help shape the beta

Use Recall during a normal session, then tell us what it got right and wrong. In Recall, choose **Report a problem** to review and send a report. Include your operating system, Ableton Live version and Recall version, what you expected, and what happened.

Screenshots and posts about Recall are welcome.

## Release notes

Each version's changes are on its [release page](https://github.com/Gavin-Berger/Recall-Releases/releases) and at [recallstudios.net/changelog](https://recallstudios.net/changelog/). Recall checks for updates itself, and you can check by hand in **Settings → What's running → Check for updates**.
